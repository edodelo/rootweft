# Code of conduct

Rootweft adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct for all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions and any other channel run by the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome people of
every background and experience level, and focus criticism on ideas and code,
never on people. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing
others' private information and sexualized language or imagery are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact
options on the GitHub profile of [@edodelo](https://github.com/edodelo), or
by opening a private security advisory if no other channel is available and
marking it as a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions and may warn,
temporarily ban or permanently ban participants, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
